# BB-Web profile

Use only after reading the bug bounty program's current written rules and activating its reviewed local policy. Confirm eligible domains, exact/wildcard distinctions, exclusions, applicable account limitations, rate controls and required identity headers. `lab_program_check` is an eligibility check, not permission to disregard program conditions.

The MCP policy does not constrain arbitrary native shell or browser operations. Before any network activity, check the actual destination and that the method is allowed. Never follow out-of-scope redirects, bypass third-party privacy, collect unnecessary personal information, or continue after program limits or unexpected sensitive data.

Reuse known case evidence and document both baseline behavior and the observed difference. Record only a minimal, verifiable account of a finding, link the underlying evidence and distinguish established impact from inference. Reports remain drafts until the user manually reviews and submits them. Avoid repeated calls that do not answer a new question.
